## 1.0.0

- Variants with `initial`, `animate`, and child propagation
- `opacity`, `x`, `y`, `scale`, `rotate`
- `delayChildren`, `staggerChildren`
- Gestures: `whileHover`, `whileTap`, `whileFocus`, `whileDrag`
- `exit` animations with `MotionPresence`
- `whileInView` and `MotionViewport`
- Spring transitions (`MotionTransitionType.spring`)
- Drag with constraints
- `layout` (animated size) and `layoutId` (via Flutter `Hero`)
- Reduced motion support
- Interactive web example gallery
